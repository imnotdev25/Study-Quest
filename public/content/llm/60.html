Stored vs used parameters. Total size has grown about 60\(\times\) since Mixtral; active size only about 8\(\times\). DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash shows its 552B backbone and decode-time 16B.

🔑 Key ideas

  • The 2023 recipe (RMSNorm, SwiGLU, RoPE, GQA) is still the skeleton of every model above.

  • 2024–2026 work is mostly about two bottlenecks: KV memory (GQA \(\to\) MLA \(\to\) compressed and FP4 caches, linear-attention hybrids) and active compute (ever finer MoE: 8 experts \(\to\) 896).

  • Stability got its own math: bounded residual mixing (mHC), bounded logits (QK-norm, softcaps, QK-clip), orthogonalized updates (Muon).
