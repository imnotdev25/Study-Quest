🚀 Starting point

One or two sentences per paper on what it contributes and where it appears in this book. Entries marked (not checked) are described from their titles and general knowledge, not from a reading for this book; check the paper before relying on details.

Foundations

  • Attention Is All You Need (2017): the transformer. Sessions 6–10.

  • Root Mean Square Layer Normalization (2019): normalise by RMS only, no mean. Architectures part; RMSNorm backward in Toolkit I.

  • Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models (2020): loss falls as a power law in parameters, data and compute. Architectures part.

  • GQA (2023): share K/V across groups of query heads; uptrain from MHA checkpoints. KV memory in this part.

  • ZeRO (2020): shard optimizer state, gradients and parameters across data-parallel ranks; the basis of FSDP. Post-training book.

  • DeepSeek-V2 (2024): MLA and DeepSeekMoE. Architectures part; MLA absorption in Toolkit II.

Serving systems

  • Orca (2022): iteration-level scheduling, the origin of continuous batching.

  • PagedAttention / vLLM (2023): paged KV blocks, sharing and copy-on-write.

  • vAttention (2024): keep the KV cache virtually contiguous with CUDA virtual memory and map physical pages on demand, so unmodified attention kernels work without a block table.

  • On Evaluating Performance of LLM Inference Serving Systems (2025): common benchmarking pitfalls, and why TTFT/TPOT averages hide stalls.

  • MegaScale-Infer (2025): disaggregated expert parallelism, attention and FFN on separate GPUs with ping-pong micro-batches.

  • Arctic Inference with Shift Parallelism (2025): switch between tensor and sequence parallelism by load.

  • From Tensor Buffer to Distributed Memory Hierarchy (not checked): by its title, treating the KV cache as a tiered, distributed memory system rather than a GPU buffer. Long-context section.

  • Prefill-as-a-Service (not checked): by its title, KV caches of next-generation (smaller-KV) models become cheap enough to move across datacentres, so prefill can be a separate service.

  • Inference economics of language models (Epoch AI, 2025): the speed–cost frontier set by batching.

  • Defeating Nondeterminism in LLM Inference (Thinking Machines, 2025): batch-dependent reduction order is the real source of nondeterminism; batch-invariant kernels.

Kernels and attention

  • FlashAttention (2022) and FlashAttention-2 (2023): IO-aware tiled attention with online softmax; better parallelism and work partitioning.

  • Ring Attention (2023): blockwise attention across a ring of devices for near-unbounded context.

  • Hardware-Efficient Attention for Fast Decoding (2025): raise decode arithmetic intensity with grouped-tied and grouped-latent attention.

  • MInference 1.0 (2024): dynamic sparse attention for long-context prefill.

  • Native Sparse Attention (DeepSeek, 2025): compressed, selected and sliding-window branches, trained end to end with hardware-aligned blocks.

  • MoBA (Moonshot, 2025): mixture of block attention; a gate picks which KV blocks each query attends to.

  • DeepSeek-V3.2 (2025): DeepSeek Sparse Attention, a lightweight indexer selecting top-\(k\) tokens. Architectures part.

  • Bridging the Gap Between Promise and Performance for Microscaling FP4 Quantization (not checked): by its title, why MXFP4/NVFP4 formats lose accuracy in practice and methods to close the gap.

Speculative decoding

  • Fast Inference from Transformers via Speculative Decoding (Leviathan et al., 2023): the exact acceptance rule derived above.

  • EAGLE-3 (2025): token-level drafting from fused multi-layer features with training-time test.

  • SuffixDecoding (2024/25): model-free speculation from suffix trees over prompts and past outputs, adaptive draft length; aimed at agentic workloads.

Test-time compute and new architectures

  • Scaling LLM Test-Time Compute Optimally (2024): allocating inference compute (search, revisions) adaptively by difficulty can beat a larger model.

  • Gated Delta Networks (2024): delta-rule linear attention with gating. Architectures part.

  • Kimi Linear (2025): Kimi Delta Attention hybridised with MLA. Architectures part.

  • Mamba-3 (not checked): the next state-space model in the Mamba line, by its title improving SSMs with state-space principles.

  • Morphing into Hybrid Attention Models (not checked): by its title, converting trained full-attention models into hybrids that mix linear or sparse layers with full attention, cutting KV memory without pretraining from scratch.

  • DeepSeek-V4 and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash: compressed and sparse attention for million-token context. Summarised in the Architectures part; details there were not checked against the technical reports.
