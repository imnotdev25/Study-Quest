🚀 Starting point

The earlier chapters are building blocks. Real models chain them, and the order matters: SFT gives the format, preference tuning gives taste, RL gives skill, and distillation passes everything to smaller models. Here are recipes from published reports.

Model post-training stages
Tulu 3 (AI2, 2024) SFT on a 939K mix \(\to\) DPO on on-policy preference pairs judged by an LLM \(\to\) RLVR on math and instruction-following with exact checkers
DeepSeek-R1 (2025) small “cold-start” SFT of long reasoning \(\to\) GRPO with rule rewards \(\to\) rejection-sample \(\approx\)600K reasoning + 200K general SFT examples from that model \(\to\) SFT again \(\to\) a second RL stage for helpfulness and harmlessness; distilled into Qwen and Llama students
SmolLM3 (HF, 2025) mid-training on reasoning traces \(\to\) SFT with /think and /no_think modes \(\to\) preference optimization (APO) \(\to\) model merging
DeepSeek-V4 (2026) per-domain expert models, each trained with SFT and GRPO \(\to\) merged into one model with on-policy distillation (model card)
DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (2026) SFT \(\to\) RL \(\to\) on-policy distillation, with reasoning effort adjustable from 1 to 100 (model card)

The two DeepSeek-V4 rows are from the model cards I read in October 2026; the others are from the published reports as I remember them.

🔑 Key ideas

  • Recipes converge: SFT \(\to\) preference \(\to\) RLVR, then distill. The newest add on-policy distillation to merge specialists.

  • Each stage’s data is mostly synthetic and filtered by the previous stage’s model.
