🚀 Starting point

Mid-training is the last part of pre-training, run with a different data mix: more math, code, textbooks and synthetic reasoning, plus some instruction-style text, during the learning-rate decay. It’s also where the context window is extended. Most 2025–2026 base models are mid-trained before any post-training starts.

✏️ Derivation: why the decay phase is the place for the best data

Step 1. During the constant-LR phase, the optimizer is noisy: each step moves the weights by about \(\eta\) in a direction dominated by gradient noise. Loss falls slowly.

Step 2. As \(\eta\to0\), the noise averages out and the weights settle into a sharper point of the basin. Most of the final loss improvement happens here, and the model is most influenced by the data it sees last.

Step 3. So the recipe is: mostly web text in the stable phase, then switch to a high-quality mix for the 10–20% decay. OLMo 2 calls this its “Dolmino” mix; SmolLM2 and SmolLM3 use several stages with growing math and code shares.

Step 4. Data mixtures: the mix is a set of weights over sources. Small proxy runs (a few hundred million parameters) are used to choose them, because mixture preferences transfer reasonably well upward in scale.

Step 5. Context extension: train on long documents with a larger RoPE base or YaRN scaling (LLM Fundamentals, Part VI, “Long Context”), usually 4K/8K \(\to\) 32K–128K over a few billion tokens. DeepSeek-V4 extends 64K \(\to\) 1M with YaRN factor 16; Kimi K3 goes 8K \(\to\) 64K in pre-training and 256K \(\to\) 1M in cooldown.
